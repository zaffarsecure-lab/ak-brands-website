6. Summary

The same short message as Order Confirmed, without the heading: order number and date, lot, product, quantity, lot rate, the view link and Trusted Firm. The total, payments, balance and transport are on the page behind the link, and that page updates by itself when you save. It never shows supplier price, supplier payments, profit or margin. Profit is worked out on the value before GST.

7. Order Confirmed on WhatsApp

Sends the customer a short "Order Confirmed" message: order number and date, lot, product, quantity, lot rate and a link. The link opens a page with everything else: total amount, token and part payments, balance and transport. It never shows invoices, PDFs, supplier details or profit. You share invoices personally, yourself. The link shows the lot number and invoice number, ends in a long secret code, and never expires. It stays active until you tap Stop sharing. The page updates by itself when you save new payments or tracking.

7b. Payment request (QR and bank details)

When the customer says "book it", send this. The customer gets a message with the amount, your bank details, and a link to a payment page for this order and lot. The page has a Pay with UPI app button (amount and lot number already filled in), your QR picture and your bank details. Nothing confirms a payment automatically: when the money arrives, tap Payment received below.

7c. Record a payment you received

For cash, UPI, bank transfer or cheque, whatever way the customer paid. It is added under Customer payments (section 3). When you save, the customer's order page and account link show it by themselves, including how it was paid. The reference stays private.

8. Delivery details for the supplier

This is the text for the parcel label. Send it to the supplier to write on the package: lot number, quantity, product, the customer's name and address, your standard phone number, and "From AK Brands Marketing". The customer's real phone number is never included. The invoices and bills you send the supplier never carry customer details.

9. Invoice and bills

Tax invoice for the customer. The sender is AK Brands Marketing. It never shows the supplier.

Tax invoice for the transporter. It shows the sender (supplier's pick-up place), the customer, and AK Brands Marketing, with a slip for the transporter to sign.

Purchase bill (URD) for the supplier. AK Brands Marketing is the customer. It never shows the real customer.

Privacy rule: the customer never sees the supplier, and the supplier never sees the customer. Only the transporter copy shows both, because the transporter needs them. Tapping a button saves the order first. The customer copy and transporter copy share one invoice number, and the supplier copy has its own URD number.

10. Links: invoices and URD bills

Link any URD bill to any invoice, even from a different order. For example, one supplier bill can feed several customers' invoices. Pick from a list and it links straight away. Nothing here is printed on the customer, transporter or supplier copies.

11. Private notes

Orders that have an invoice or URD bill number cannot be deleted. Set the status to Cancelled instead.
