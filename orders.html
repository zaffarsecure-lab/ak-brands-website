6. Summary

The message uses the same layout as Order Confirmed: order number and date, lot, product, quantity, lot rate, total amount, token and part payments, balance, transport details, the view link, and Trusted Firm. It never shows supplier price, supplier payments, profit or margin. Profit is worked out on the value before GST.

7. Order Confirmed on WhatsApp

Sends the customer an "Order Confirmed" message with lot number, product, quantity, rate, total amount, token and part payments, and a link. The link opens a page with the order details only. It never shows invoices, PDFs, supplier details or profit. You share invoices personally, yourself. The link shows the lot number and invoice number, ends in a long secret code, and never expires. It stays active until you tap Stop sharing. The page updates by itself when you save new payments or tracking.

9. Invoice and bills

Tax invoice for the customer. The sender is AK Brands Marketing. It never shows the supplier.

Tax invoice for the transporter. It shows the sender (supplier's pick-up place), the customer, and AK Brands Marketing, with a slip for the transporter to sign.

Purchase bill (URD) for the supplier. AK Brands Marketing is the customer. It never shows the real customer.

Privacy rule: the customer never sees the supplier, and the supplier never sees the customer. Only the transporter copy shows both, because the transporter needs them. Tapping a button saves the order first. The customer copy and transporter copy share one invoice number, and the supplier copy has its own URD number.

10. Links: invoices and URD bills

Link any URD bill to any invoice, even from a different order. For example, one supplier bill can feed several customers' invoices. Pick from a list and it links straight away. Nothing here is printed on the customer, transporter or supplier copies.

11. Private notes

Orders that have an invoice or URD bill number cannot be deleted. Set the status to Cancelled instead.
